Expand description

One reciprocal summand T(q) of the sum, without the Bloch phase exp(-i q . r) that the caller multiplies in. Every sum shares the argument v = (q . q / k^2 - 1) / (2 eta^2); wave and dim select the sum. 1D spherical sums, whose odd Kambe orders cancel by up to e^(w^2) at w = k rho eta, add the rounding bounds of its value and derivatives in units of epsilon to bound; the other sums keep their relative accuracy.
