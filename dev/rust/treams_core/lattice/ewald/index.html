The Ewald sum: real-space part, reciprocal-space part and self term.

Upstream: lsum*, realsum* and recsum* of lattice/_esum.pyx. The reduction into a cell, the exact zeros by symmetry, the checks at small splits and the spectral series of 1D spherical sums are treams-rs extensions.
