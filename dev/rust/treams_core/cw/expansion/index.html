Module expansion 

Source
Expand description

Cylindrical expansion matrices between bases and over periodic arrays, with analytic pullbacks. Entries that share a CouplingKey share one coefficient, evaluated once.

Upstream: the treams.Expand and treams.ExpandLattice operators between cylindrical bases.
