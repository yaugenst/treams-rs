The degree recurrence DLMF 8.8.2 from E1 (integer degree) or sqrt(pi) erfc(sqrt z) (half-integer degree), with powers of z updated by one multiplication per step (integer degrees stay real on the real axis), and a first-order bound on the absolute error in units of epsilon from the start value and each endpoint term. modulus is |z|.
