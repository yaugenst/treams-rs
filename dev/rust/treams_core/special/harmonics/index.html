Module harmonics 

Source
Expand description

Cartesian solid harmonics r^l P_l^m(z/r) e^(i m phi) with their gradients and Hessians, singly or tabulated for every degree and order, their values and tangential gradients on the unit sphere, and the normalization of spherical harmonics.

treams-rs extension. Solid harmonics are polynomials in x, y, z, so they and their derivatives stay finite on the polar axis and at the origin. Spherical translations, lattice sums and field evaluations use them.
