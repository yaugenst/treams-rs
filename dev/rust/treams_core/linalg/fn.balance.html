fn balance(

Expand description

Even out the row and column sums of a max-equilibrated operator in place with power-of-two factors, folded into row and column.

Row and column maxima do not determine the scales: in A = D_r H D_c the largest entry of a row depends on D_c, so an off-diagonal entry of H can end up as large as its diagonal, partial pivoting may choose it, and a change of units costs digits. Unit magnitude sums determine the scaled operator whenever it has total support (Sinkhorn and Knopp), whatever the units.

Each sweep moves every column factor, then every row factor, to a power of two that minimizes the convex potential Σ |a_ij| x_i y_j − Σ ln x_i − Σ ln y_j along it. The sweeps stop after one that moves no factor by more than two, or after BALANCE_SWEEPS. A sum that is not normal, or factors beyond 2^±256, keep the max scales.
