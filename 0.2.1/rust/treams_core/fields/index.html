Cartesian spherical and cylindrical vector waves, field sums and field operators.

field sums a mode expansion at sample points, and operator builds the matrix that maps mode coefficients to those fields; both have analytic gradients.

The waves use Cartesian components built from solid harmonics, so they stay finite on the z axis and at the expansion centre, where polar coordinates are singular.

Upstream: treams.efield and the vector waves treams.special.vsw_* and vcw_*.
