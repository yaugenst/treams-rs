Module polarization

Expand description

Polarization conventions of vector waves: how the polarization index pol combines the two multipole parts M and N of a wave.

  • Parity: pol = 0 is M and pol = 1 is N.
  • Helicity: (N + (2 pol - 1) M) / sqrt(2), so pol = 0 is negative and pol = 1 positive helicity.

Upstream: the convention of treams.special.vsw_A and vcw_A. polarized_wave applies it to vector waves, and legendre::polarized_angular applies the same sign 2 pol - 1 (helicity_sign) to the angular factors tau + (2 pol - 1) pi of plane-wave expansions.
