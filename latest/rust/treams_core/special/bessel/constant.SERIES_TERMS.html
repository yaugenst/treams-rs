const SERIES_TERMS: u32 = 16;
Expand description

Terms of the small-argument power series of spherical_radial and cylindrical_radial. Below |z| = SERIES_RADIUS (0.5) each term is at most 1/(16 q^2) of its predecessor, so the last one is below 1e-40 of the first.
