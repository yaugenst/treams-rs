treams_core::cluster

Module spheres 

Source
Expand description

Dense clusters of homogeneous nonmagnetic spheres in vacuum, with gradients of the radii, permittivities and positions.

Upstream: treams.TMatrix.cluster of TMatrix.sphere T-matrices, then interaction.solve(). The gradients and the reusable factor of sphere_cluster_factor are treams-rs extensions.
