Expand description

sum_n factor (2 eta^2)^n F_(1/2 - n) P(n) over the half-integer orders n = 0..=orders of one reciprocal point with the degree polynomials P (zero ones skipped). Below every automatic split the orders weigh their terms, and are summed again from the single Kambe integrals where those bound the sum more tightly (Reduced::prefers_single).
