Cotangents of the inputs of fresnel, in the order of its arguments; each array lists the medium on the negative side (below), then the positive side (above).

What fresnel saves for its pullback: the wavenumbers, the normal wavenumbers and the impedances. The pullback differentiates the closed forms again instead of keeping a Jacobian.
