treams_core::special

Module coordinates

Coordinate and vector-component transformations in orthonormal local frames.

Upstream: treams.special.car2cyl, car2sph, cyl2car, cyl2sph, sph2car, sph2cyl, car2pol, pol2car (point) and their v-prefixed vector twins such as vcar2sph (vector), from _coord.pyx. Spherical coordinates are (r, theta, phi) with the polar angle theta, cylindrical ones (rho, phi, z), as in treams.
