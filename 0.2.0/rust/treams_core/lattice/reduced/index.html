Reduced integrals F_n(v, t) of the reciprocal terms.

Upstream: _redincgamma and _redintkambe of lattice/_esum.pyx. The series in t, the continuation to other sheets and the rounding bounds are treams-rs extensions.

Structs§

Reduced 🔒
Reduced integrals F_n(v, t) of one reciprocal point, as jets.

Constants§

HALF_INTEGER_SERIES_T 🔒
Largest |t| at which Reduced sums the half-integer orders from their series in t (which loses up to e^|t| ulps) instead of the Kambe chain (whose correlated errors the sums cancel less the smaller |t|).
SERIES_REACH 🔒
Largest |t| at which Reduced takes the series for the integer orders below the base pair of the Kambe recurrence (2n < -2), which need a series of gamma functions of their own each, while the series in t takes all orders from one ladder.
SERIES_T 🔒
Largest |t| = |w|^2 at which Reduced sums every integer order of F_n(v, t) from its series in t: the Kambe integrals take eta = -i / w, whose powers leave the range of f64 from about |w| = 1e-50, and the series terms alternate at most like those of e^-|t|, losing a few ulps up to SERIES_REACH.
SERIES_TERMS 🔒
Terms of the series in t of Reduced at most; |t| <= SERIES_REACH stops within 20.

on_sheet 🔒

z^n / (-z)^n on the principal branches, for z with a signed imaginary part: (-1)^n, or for half-integer n (-1)^floor(n) times -i on and below the real axis and i above it.
reduced_gamma 🔒

reduced_power 🔒
